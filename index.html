Establishing the Foundation

In this session, we transition away from the traditional real estate agent grind. To succeed with **The Vault Protocol**, you must stop thinking about the next commission check and start thinking about long-term asset acquisition.

The "Steele" Realization:

"A commission feeds you for a month. Equity feeds your family for a generation. Most people are too busy chasing the month to see the generation."

Your First Mission

Before we touch the scraper technology, you must define your Freedom Number. This is the exact monthly passive income required to cover all personal expenses and allow you to work on assets full-time.
